# Favorite Series/Movies
1. Haikyu!!
   - A japanese series about volleyball that has manga and anime adaptation. It's about Shoyo Hinata's love of volleyball, who dreams of becoming a top athlete despite his short height. After joining Karasuno High School’s volleyball team, he teams up with the talented but hot-headed Tobio Kageyama. Together they strive to reach the national stage, learning teamwork, determination, and friendship along the way.
2. A Promised Neverland
   - A japanese series that is a dark fantasy anime about a group of orphans living happily in an isolated orphanage, until they discover the horrifying truth behind their home. There are three gifted kids who are Emma, Norman, and Ray, they look for a way to escape from their evil caretaker and lead the other children in a risky escape plan.
3. Demon slayer
   - A japanese series is about the story of Tanjiro Kamado, a boy who fights demons after his family’s tragic death. With his sister Nezuko, who has become a demon herself, he sets out to become a demon slayer to avenge his family and cure his sister.
4. Your Name
   - A japanese movie about two teenagers named Mitsuha and Taki who mysteriously begin swapping bodies. Its a romance fantasy as they experience each other’s lives, they form a deep bond and work together to change fate itself. But things become even more complicated when the boy and girl decide to meet in person.
